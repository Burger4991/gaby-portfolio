Which should be the default when someone visits the site for the first time?

Dark default

Most visitors land on the dark version. Feels more cinematic and editorial. Better at night, better on OLED screens. Sets a strong first impression.

Light default

Most visitors land on the warm ivory. Feels more approachable and tactile. Fashion work pops on light backgrounds. Better for daytime viewing and print.
